# Growth artwork

Built-in image_gen was used to generate two distinct age illustrations using room.png as the identity reference. Adult art remains the original room.png. The generated PNGs have RGB checkerboard backgrounds; the app uses dedicated SVG silhouette masks in catSprites, with a small inset and exclusions between the body and tail to display only each cat. The source PNGs are kept unmodified. Face anchors are set independently for each stage in style.css.

## baby.png prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one transparent PNG sprite for the Japanese children's pet game Makopet. Input image is REFERENCE ONLY: preserve the identity, orange-tan tabby markings, cream white muzzle and chest, warm brown eyes, pink inner ears, gentle children's book watercolor texture of the central cat. Output ONLY one full-body cat on genuinely transparent background (alpha), no room, no floor, no shadow, no text, no clothes or accessories. Centered front-facing sitting cat, tail curls along its RIGHT side, whole body visible with small clear margin, ears at top and feet at bottom. Straight level head so separately overlaid glasses and hat align. Depict the BABY life stage: visibly very young tiny baby kitten, huge round head about 60 percent of total height, round chubby cheeks, very short stubby legs and paws, small rounded ears, tiny round body, curious wide brown eyes. Visibly different from simply shrinking an adult. High-quality soft painted sprite consistent with reference.

## kitten.png prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one transparent PNG sprite for the Japanese children's pet game Makopet. Input image is REFERENCE ONLY: preserve the identity, orange-tan tabby markings, cream white muzzle and chest, warm brown eyes, pink inner ears, gentle children's book watercolor texture of the central cat. Output ONLY one full-body cat on genuinely transparent background (alpha), no room, no floor, no shadow, no text, no clothes or accessories. Centered front-facing sitting cat, tail curls along its RIGHT side, whole body visible with small clear margin, ears at top and feet at bottom. Straight level head so separately overlaid glasses and hat align. Depict the JUVENILE KITTEN life stage: intermediate between baby and the reference adult, playful alert upright sitting posture, head about 48 percent of total height, moderately long legs compared with baby but shorter than adult, slightly fluffy rounded body, perked ears, youthful brown eyes. Preserve same orange tabby individual. High-quality soft painted sprite consistent with reference.
